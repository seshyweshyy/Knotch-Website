The point of it

Built to feel built in.

Knotch is a personal adaptation of boring.notch, reworked around one obsession: the little details that separate something that fits from something that just works.

Native materials.

Frosted glass, real vibrancy, and Liquid Glass on macOS 26. Nothing painted on — the same materials macOS itself is made of.

Motion with manners.

Sneak peeks that appear when a track changes and dissolve when you look away. Animation that informs, never performs.

Open, entirely.

GPLv3, on GitHub, with every release scanned on VirusTotal. Read the code, fork it, make it yours.

Questions?

What Mac models does Knotch support?

Any MacBook Pro from late 2021 onwards and the M2 MacBook Air and later — the ones with a physical notch. It also runs on older Macs and external displays; the notch-area UI won't appear, but the music controls, shelf, and other widgets still work.

What version of macOS do I need?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Liquid Glass features need macOS 26.

Which music apps does Knotch support?

Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music get album art, track info, playback controls, and a progress bar. Anything else that exposes a Now Playing session shows basic track info.

Why does macOS say the app can't be opened?

Knotch isn't notarized with Apple yet, so Gatekeeper blocks it on first launch. Right-click Knotch.app → Open → Open once, and it launches normally after that.

First launch on macOS

Knotch isn't notarized yet, so macOS will block it the first time. Run this once in Terminal, then open it normally:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Knotch.app

Or open the app, dismiss the warning, and choose Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
